HOW TO PLAY
YOU vs CPU

Take turns against the CPU. Correct consonants keep your turn; misses, BANKRUPT, and LOSE TURN pass play to your opponent. The first to solve a puzzle earns their round bank. The CPU spins, guesses letters, and buys vowels automatically.

Spin first to earn money for each matching consonant. After a spin, pick one consonant. Each vowel costs $250 from your round bank and doesn't require a spin. You can solve at any time.

BANKRUPT wipes your round bank. LOSE TURN ends that spin. Both pass the turn to your opponent. Incorrect consonants also pass the turn. Solve correctly to bank your round winnings; a wrong solution costs $250 and passes the turn.
