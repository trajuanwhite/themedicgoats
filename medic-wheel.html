HOW TO PLAY
How to play

Spin first to earn money for each matching consonant. After a spin, pick one consonant. Each vowel costs $250 from your round bank and doesn't require a spin. You can solve at any time.

BANKRUPT wipes your round bank. LOSE TURN ends that spin. Both let you try again. Incorrect consonants end that spin. Complete puzzles to secure the round bank toward your total.
