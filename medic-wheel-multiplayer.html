    How to play

    Players take turns spinning and guessing consonants. Correct consonants earn money for each matching letter and let you go again. Wrong guesses pass the turn. Vowels cost $250 from your round bank.

    Bankrupt clears your round bank. Lose Turn passes play to the next contestant. Solve a puzzle to bank your round earnings. Only the host advances to the next puzzle.

    Late players can join the room while a round is active and enter the turn rotation.
