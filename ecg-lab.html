Electrode placement & lead derivation

Einthoven's triangle

Einthoven's law: Lead II = Lead I + Lead III. The arrows run from each bipolar lead's negative electrode toward its positive electrode.

Frontal-plane QRS axis

Normal−30° to +90°
Left deviation−90° to −30°
Right deviation+90° to +180°
Extreme deviation−180° to −90°

Normal adult QRS axis: −30° to +90°. If I is positive and aVF negative, check lead II to distinguish normal leftward axis from left-axis deviation. Near-perpendicular leads may be nearly isoelectric.
