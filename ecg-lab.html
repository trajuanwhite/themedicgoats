Model limitations: This is a simplified educational vector-dipole model, not a patient ECG or validated heart–torso forward model. It mathematically derives I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF and V1–V6 from shared virtual electrode potentials. It does not accurately predict clinical abnormalities, amplitude calibration, or individual anatomy. Not for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical training assessment.

Electrode placement & lead derivation

A standard 12-lead ECG uses 10 electrodes. Tap an electrode to learn its placement, or select a lead to see which electrodes contribute to its voltage calculation.

Checks Einthoven's law and augmented-lead sum across the example beat.

Placement map is schematic. Arm and leg markers indicate limb electrode locations rather than exact distances. Positions correspond to a standard diagnostic ECG, not torso-modified monitoring.

Placement references: AHA/ACCF/HRS ECG standardization · GE Healthcare lead placement.
