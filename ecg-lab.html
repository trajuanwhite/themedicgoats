Model limitations: This is a simplified educational vector-dipole model, not a patient ECG or validated heart–torso forward model. It mathematically derives I, II, III, aVR, aVL, aVF and V1–V6 from shared virtual electrode potentials. It does not accurately predict clinical abnormalities, amplitude calibration, or individual anatomy. Not for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical training assessment.
